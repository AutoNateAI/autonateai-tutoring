# AutonateAI 90-Day Scaling Plan

## Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-30)

### Week 1-2: Brand Infrastructure

- Finalize brand identity (logo, color palette, typography)
- Set up e-commerce store (Shopify recommended for merch)
- Create brand style guide for all content and merch designs
- Set up social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, X/Twitter, LinkedIn)
- Build an email list landing page with a lead magnet (free AI resource or wallpaper pack)
- Register domain and set up basic SEO structure

### Week 3-4: Content Engine Setup

- Batch-create 30 days of short-form video content
- Content pillars:
  - AI education / tips
  - Behind-the-scenes design process
  - Tech culture / memes / relatable dev life
  - Product teasers and lifestyle shots
- Set up a content calendar (Notion or Trello)
- Film 4-6 "founder story" clips explaining the AutonateAI mission
- Write 2-3 SEO blog posts targeting long-tail keywords:
  - "Best AI developer apparel"
  - "Tech t-shirts for women in coding"
  - "AI engineer aesthetic clothing"

### Milestone Targets (Day 30)

- 500+ email subscribers
- 1,000+ combined social followers
- Store live with 3-5 initial products
- 30 pieces of content published

---

## Phase 2: Launch & Traction (Days 31-60)

### Week 5-6: First Official Drop

- Launch limited collection (3-5 designs)
- Create urgency: 72-hour drop window or limited quantity (50-100 per design)
- Email blast to subscriber list
- Coordinate social media blitz across all platforms
- Run a giveaway: "Follow + tag 2 friends + share = win free merch"
- Post daily countdown content leading to drop

### Week 7-8: Influencer & Community Push

- Identify 10-15 micro-influencers in tech/dev/AI space (5K-50K followers)
- Send free merch to 5 influencers in exchange for content
- Launch Discord community for AutonateAI insiders
  - Channels: general, merch-drops, ai-projects, feedback
- Partner with 1-2 coding bootcamps or AI communities for cross-promotion
- Host a Twitter/X Space or Instagram Live: "Building an AI Brand from Scratch"
- Start collecting and reposting UGC (user-generated content)

### Milestone Targets (Day 60)

- First 100+ orders fulfilled
- 2,500+ combined social followers
- 1,500+ email subscribers
- 5+ pieces of UGC from customers
- Discord community with 200+ members

---

## Phase 3: Scale & Optimize (Days 61-90)

### Week 9-10: Paid Advertising

- Launch Meta ads (Instagram/Facebook)
  - Start with $20-30/day budget
  - Test 3 ad creatives: lifestyle, product-focused, founder story
  - Target: tech professionals, coding bootcamp grads, AI enthusiasts, 18-35
- Launch TikTok Spark Ads (boost best-performing organic content)
  - Start with $15-20/day
- Set up retargeting campaigns for website visitors who didn't purchase
- A/B test landing pages for conversion optimization

### Week 11-12: Revenue Multipliers

- Launch second merch drop with new designs based on customer feedback
- Introduce bundles:
  - Merch + Mini AI Course
  - Merch + Exclusive Discord Access
  - Merch + Digital Asset Pack (wallpapers, stickers)
- Start an affiliate/referral program (10-15% commission)
- Pitch to tech newsletters for features
- Apply to tech conferences and hackathons for vendor/sponsor spots
- Explore wholesale partnerships with coding bootcamps

### Milestone Targets (Day 90)

- 500+ total orders
- 5,000+ combined social followers
- 3,000+ email subscribers
- Positive ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) on paid campaigns
- 2 successful merch drops completed
- Revenue baseline established for next quarter planning

## Scaling Triggers (When to Accelerate)

Move to aggressive scaling when:

- Organic content consistently gets 10K+ views
- Conversion rate is above 2.5%
- CAC is below $15
- You have 20+ pieces of UGC
- Email open rates are above 30%
- Discord community is actively engaged daily

When these triggers hit, double ad spend, increase drop frequency, and explore new product categories (hoodies, hats, stickers, laptop sleeves).
