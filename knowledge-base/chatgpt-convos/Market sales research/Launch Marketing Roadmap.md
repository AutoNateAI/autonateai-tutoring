# AutonateAI Launch Marketing Roadmap

## Pre-Launch Phase (2 Weeks Before Drop)

### Brand Awareness Buildup

**Day -14 to Day -8:**

- Post "something is coming" teaser on all socials
- Share behind-the-scenes clips of the design process
- Post a founder video: "Why I'm building AutonateAI"
- Start an email waitlist with early access incentive
- Tease one design element per day (close-up fabric shots, logo reveal, color palette)

**Day -7 to Day -1:**

- Reveal full product lineup via carousel posts
- Post "7 days until drop" countdown content daily
- Share the brand story and mission in long-form (LinkedIn article, Instagram caption)
- Send email #1 to waitlist: "You're on the list. Here's what's dropping."
- Go live on Instagram or TikTok to talk about the brand and answer questions
- Seed 3-5 influencers with product so they can post on launch day
- Create a TikTok/Reel: "What if AI engineers had their own apparel brand?"

---

## Launch Day (Day 0)

### The Drop

**Morning (8-10 AM):**

- Send launch email to full list: "WE'RE LIVE. Shop now."
- Post launch announcement across all platforms simultaneously
- Pin launch post on Instagram and TikTok
- Update link in bio to point directly to the shop
- Post Instagram Stories every 2 hours with product highlights

**Midday (12-2 PM):**

- Share real-time updates: "50 shirts sold in 3 hours" (social proof)
- Repost any influencer content that goes live
- Engage with every comment and DM personally
- Post a "thank you" video if early sales momentum is strong

**Evening (6-9 PM):**

- Post a "last chance" reminder if running a limited drop
- Go live to celebrate and thank early supporters
- Send email #2: "The response has been insane. Don't miss out."

## Post-Launch Phase (Days 1-14)

### Week 1: Momentum & Social Proof

**Content Strategy:**

- Day 1: Post sales milestone update ("We sold out of X in 24 hours")
- Day 2: Customer spotlight / first order shoutout
- Day 3: Behind-the-scenes of packing and shipping orders
- Day 4: Repost UGC (customer photos wearing merch)
- Day 5: "Why we designed this" deep-dive on hero product
- Day 6: Meme or relatable dev content wearing the merch
- Day 7: Recap post: "Week 1 in numbers"

**Email Sequence:**

- Day 2: Order confirmation + "Share your fit" CTA
- Day 4: "Your order is on its way" + referral code
- Day 7: "How to style your AutonateAI gear" lookbook email

### Week 2: Extend the Wave

- Launch a "restock" or "encore drop" if items sold out
- Run a giveaway: "Tag a friend who codes + follow = win"
- Publish a blog post: "The Story Behind AutonateAI's First Drop"
- Collect and publish customer testimonials
- Start planning Drop #2 based on feedback and sales data

## Ongoing Marketing Channels

### Organic Social (Daily)

| Platform | Content Type | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | Short-form video (15-60s) | 1-2x daily |
| Instagram Reels | Lifestyle + product content | 1x daily |
| Instagram Stories | Behind-the-scenes, polls, Q&A | 3-5x daily |
| X/Twitter | Hot takes, memes, community engagement | 3-5x daily |
| LinkedIn | Founder journey, brand story, industry insights | 2-3x weekly |

### Content Pillars

1. **Product Content** (30%) - New designs, close-ups, flat lays, on-body shots
2. **Culture Content** (30%) - Dev memes, AI hot takes, "day in the life" of builders
3. **Education Content** (20%) - AI tips, coding tutorials, tech news commentary
4. **Brand Story Content** (10%) - Founder journey, mission, behind-the-scenes
5. **Community Content** (10%) - Customer features, UGC reposts, shoutouts

### Email Marketing

| Email Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Newsletter | Weekly | Value content + soft product mentions |
| Drop Announcements | Per drop | Drive urgency and sales |
| Welcome Sequence | Automated (3 emails) | Onboard new subscribers, tell brand story |
| Abandoned Cart | Automated | Recover lost sales |
| Post-Purchase | Automated (2 emails) | Thank you + referral program |

## Drop Strategy (Recurring)

### The Hype Cycle for Each Drop

```
Week -2:  Tease ("Something new is cooking...")
Week -1:  Reveal (Show designs, build anticipation)
Day 0:    Drop (Limited window or limited quantity)
Week +1:  Social proof (UGC, testimonials, sold-out alerts)
Week +2:  Encore/Restock (if demand warrants it)
Week +3:  Cool down (regular content, community building)
Week +4:  Begin teasing next drop
```

### Recommended Drop Cadence

- **Monthly drops** for the first 6 months to build consistency
- Each drop should have a theme or story tied to AI/tech culture
- Example themes:
  - Drop 1: "Genesis" - The founding collection
  - Drop 2: "Dark Mode" - All-black developer aesthetic
  - Drop 3: "Syntax Error" - Bold, glitchy, rebellious designs
  - Drop 4: "Neural Network" - Interconnected community collection
  - Drop 5: "Open Source" - Collaborative designs with community input
  - Drop 6: "Deploy" - Premium/elevated line

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low launch day traffic | Pre-built email list + influencer coordination |
| Inventory issues | Start with print-on-demand or small batch |
| Negative feedback on designs | Pre-launch surveys with email list |
| Shipping delays | Clear communication + tracking updates |
| Ad account issues | Build organic foundation first, ads are supplementary |
| Low engagement | Focus on community building over broadcasting |
