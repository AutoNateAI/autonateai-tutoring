# AutoNateAI Workshop Portal Landing Page Brief

Design a landing page for the AutoNateAI Workshop Portal.

This is not a generic AI course. It is a guided student operating system for learning how to use AI tools, structured thinking systems, and workbook-based workflows to reduce overwhelm and perform better in school and life.

## Core Offer

The product is a one-time purchase of `$129`.

No subscription framing.

Position it as a one-time unlock into a new way of operating, not just content access.

## What The Portal Actually Is

The portal is an interactive, narrative-driven learning experience.

It teaches students how to move from overload and chaos into structure and leverage.

It includes:

1. A narrated cinematic slide deck with voiceover and visuals
2. A storyline featuring a friend group of students navigating pressure, overload, school, future planning, and AI adoption
3. Practical instruction on installing and using AI coding agents / AI tools
4. "Thinking Systems" that help students turn messy cognitive work into structured repeatable systems
5. Spreadsheet/workbook-based workflows that make thinking visible and actionable

## The Emotional Problem We Are Selling Against

Students are not failing because they are lazy or unintelligent.

They are overloaded.

They are buried in assignments, deadlines, decisions, pressure from school, college, work, family, and social comparison.

The portal reframes the problem:

**The gap is not intelligence. It is infrastructure.**

## Big Idea

This portal gives students the infrastructure.

It teaches them how to think with AI, not just ask random prompts.

It gives them systems for planning, studying, reading, writing, and reflecting.

It makes AI feel practical, empowering, and emotionally relieving.

## Who This Is For

Primarily high school and early college students.

Especially students who are smart, capable, ambitious, and overwhelmed.

Students juggling:

- classes
- homework
- college decisions
- work
- family obligations
- inconsistent motivation
- too much information
- weak structure

This is also for students who feel behind, disorganized, or cognitively overloaded but still want to win.

## Transformation

Before:

- scattered
- stressed
- reactive
- behind
- drowning in tabs, assignments, and mental clutter
- using AI randomly, if at all

After:

- structured
- calm
- strategic
- faster
- clearer
- using AI as leverage
- operating with systems instead of panic

## Important Product Language

Call them **Thinking Systems**, not prompt packs.

These are reusable systems for specific types of student pain.

Examples of Thinking Systems:

- Daily Time Grid
- Assignment Sprint Planner
- Reading Capture Matrix
- Study Heatmap Board
- Paper Source Matrix
- Day Debrief Lab

How to frame those:

Each system should feel like an unlocked ability or tool for a specific problem.

For example:

- when your day feels impossible
- when a big assignment feels too vague
- when you read and nothing sticks
- when everything feels equally urgent
- when your sources are scattered
- when you keep repeating bad patterns

## Visual Direction

Do not design this like generic edtech.

Do not make it look like basic SaaS cards on white.

It should feel premium, cinematic, and intentional.

It can feel like a mix of:

- dramatic student future
- AI leverage
- structure emerging from chaos
- narrated digital experience

Good visual motifs:

- glowing systems
- structured grids emerging from mess
- layered school pressure
- dashboards, sheets, maps, nodes, planning structures
- transformation from chaos to order

Avoid:

- stock graduation clichés
- generic robots
- corporate blue SaaS sameness
- cliché "AI head with circuits" visuals
