The AI Library

Welcome to the AutoNateAI AI Library.

This library is organized around the three audiences the site now serves best:

  • Students building confidence with AI workflows and structured problem solving.
  • Researchers turning messy knowledge into repeatable systems and clear outputs.
  • Professionals designing practical AI systems they can use in real work.

Each library is split into Easy, Medium, and Hard so people can enter at the right level and grow into stronger systems thinking over time.

How To Use The Library​

  • Start with the audience library that matches your current role.
  • Pick a difficulty level based on how much structure and technical depth you want.
  • Use the quest pages as guided builds, not just reading material.
