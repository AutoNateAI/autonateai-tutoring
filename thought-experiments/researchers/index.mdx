# Researchers

This library is for researchers who need better structure, cleaner knowledge flows, and stronger ways to organize complex information.

## What Is In This Library

- **Easy:** foundational workflows for turning notes and sources into usable structure.
- **Medium:** synthesis patterns for connecting ideas, evidence, and research questions.
- **Hard:** advanced system-building for larger research pipelines and collaborative knowledge work.

## Difficulty Levels

## 🟢 [Easy](/thought-experiments/researchers/Easy/)
Build the habits and frameworks that make research work easier to organize.

## 🟡 [Medium](/thought-experiments/researchers/Medium/)
Move into multi-step synthesis, relationship mapping, and more deliberate workflow design.

## 🔴 [Hard](/thought-experiments/researchers/Hard/)
Prepare for larger-scale research systems and more demanding orchestration.
