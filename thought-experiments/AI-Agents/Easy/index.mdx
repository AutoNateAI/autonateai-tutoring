# Applied Intelligence for Operators

**Level:** Easy
**Target:** Local Shops and Small Businesses

Experiments in this category focus on the "Figma → AI → Sheets" factory. We show local founders how to turn manual, headache-inducing tasks into automated command centers.

### Key Workflows:
- **Inventory Automation:** Tracking stock levels via voice-to-sheet.
- **Sales Intelligence:** Turning raw transactions into visual trend reports.
- **Operational Command Centers:** Mapping business workflows in Figma and deploying them as intelligent spreadsheets.
