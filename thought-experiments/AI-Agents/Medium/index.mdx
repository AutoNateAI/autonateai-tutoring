# Agent Orchestration for Researchers

**Level:** Medium
**Target:** Researchers and Academic Professionals

Move beyond static search. These experiments focus on building autonomous loops that act as research assistants, processing massive datasets and synthesizing insights.

### Key Workflows:
- **Paper to Matrix:** Automatically extracting claims and evidence from Archive.org into structured synthesis matrices.
- **Knowledge Topology Mapping:** Using AI to visualize the relationships between disparate research concepts.
- **Multimodal Extraction:** Processing video lectures and research talks into searchable knowledge bases.
